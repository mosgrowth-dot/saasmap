HubSpot vs Monday CRM (2026):
Which Platform Fits Your Team?

Purpose-built CRM vs versatile Work OS — who wins for sales teams, and when does Monday CRM make more sense than HubSpot?

HubSpot vs Monday CRM: The Short Version

HubSpot is the better CRM for dedicated sales and marketing teams. Its purpose-built sales automation, email sequences, marketing hub, and deep reporting make it the more powerful CRM platform for B2B companies focused on pipeline and growth.

Monday CRM shines for teams that blend sales with project management and collaboration. If your salespeople double as account managers and project coordinators, Monday's visual work OS with CRM features can replace multiple tools in one flexible platform.

HubSpot → Best for dedicated sales & marketing teams Monday CRM → Best for collaborative teams mixing sales + projects

Overview: Different DNA, Overlapping Use Cases

HubSpot and Monday.com both have CRM products, but they arrived at CRM from completely different directions. Understanding that origin story is key to making the right choice.

HubSpot was born as a marketing and CRM platform. Its Sales Hub is a natural evolution of a company whose entire mission is helping businesses attract, engage, and delight customers. Every feature — email sequences, lead scoring, conversation intelligence, attribution reporting — was purpose-designed for sales and marketing teams.

Monday.com started as a Work OS: a flexible project management and collaboration platform. Its monday CRM is built on the same infrastructure, which makes it highly customizable and visually appealing, but it doesn't have the same depth of sales-specific functionality that HubSpot has built over a decade.

Monday CRM is genuinely useful — don't dismiss it as "just a project tool pretending to be a CRM." Over 186,000+ companies use Monday.com for customer-facing workflows. But the question is: does your team need a purpose-built sales engine, or a flexible work platform that also handles deals?

💡 The key question: Is your team primarily doing sales outreach and pipeline management? → HubSpot. Or does your team blend client acquisition, account management, and project delivery in the same workflow? → Monday CRM might be the smarter choice.

CRM Features Deep Dive

Pipeline Management

Both platforms offer excellent visual pipeline management. HubSpot's pipeline is designed specifically for B2B sales — it handles lead/contact/company relationships cleanly, automatically logs emails and activities, and provides deal rotation, win probability, and custom properties. Monday CRM's pipeline is built on their flexible board system, which is highly customizable but requires more setup to mirror the depth of HubSpot's sales-specific features.

Email & Outreach

HubSpot wins clearly on email functionality. Sales Hub Professional includes full multi-step sequences (automated email + task chains that pause when a prospect replies), open/click tracking, templates, scheduling, and AI-suggested send times. Monday CRM (Pro plan) includes basic email sending and tracking, but the sequencing and automation depth doesn't match HubSpot's.

Reporting & Analytics

HubSpot's reporting suite (Professional+) is purpose-built for sales: funnel analytics, deal velocity, activity reports, rep performance dashboards, and multi-touch attribution. Monday CRM's dashboards are beautiful and flexible, but they lack HubSpot's sales-specific pre-built reports and deeper attribution modeling.

Contact & Lead Enrichment

HubSpot has a significant edge with its data enrichment capabilities — it automatically enriches contact and company records with data from its database of 25M+ companies. Monday CRM requires manual data entry or third-party integrations for enrichment. For outbound sales teams, this data enrichment advantage is meaningful.

🏆
Winner: CRM Features — HubSpot

HubSpot is a more mature, purpose-built CRM with deeper sales automation, email sequencing, and reporting. Monday CRM is flexible and visual, but sales-specific depth isn't its core strength.

Automation & AI

HubSpot Automation

HubSpot's workflow automation (Professional+) is one of the most powerful no-code automation engines in the CRM space. You can trigger automations based on virtually any CRM event (deal stage change, email open, form submission, contact property change) and execute complex multi-step sequences involving emails, tasks, notifications, property updates, and webhook calls. Plus, HubSpot's marketing automation (email campaigns, lead nurturing flows) is natively integrated.

Monday CRM Automation

Monday.com's automation builder is visual, intuitive, and genuinely good for standard use cases. The Standard plan includes 250 automation actions/month, Pro includes 25,000/month. "When [trigger], then [action]" logic covers most team needs. However, automation complexity is more limited than HubSpot — especially for multi-step sales sequences and marketing automation.

AI: Monday AI vs HubSpot Breeze

Monday AI (available on Pro+): Summarizes updates and meeting notes, extracts action items, suggests formula column values, and provides natural language search across boards. Useful for productivity but not deep sales intelligence.

HubSpot Breeze: Encompasses content generation for sales emails and marketing, predictive lead scoring, conversation intelligence (call transcription), deal health insights, and the Breeze Copilot chat interface for querying CRM data. More comprehensive for sales and marketing workflows.

🏆
Winner: Automation & AI — HubSpot

HubSpot's automation and AI capabilities are significantly deeper for sales and marketing use cases. Monday's automation is solid for workflow management but doesn't match HubSpot's breadth of sales-specific intelligence.

Project Management & Collaboration

This is Monday.com's home turf — and it shows. monday CRM is built on the same platform as monday Work OS, meaning you get world-class project management features alongside your CRM:

  • WorkDocs: Collaborative documents embedded directly in your boards
  • Multiple views: Kanban, timeline (Gantt), calendar, chart, map, form views — all available for any board
  • @mentions and updates: Contextual team communication within deal/project records
  • Time tracking: Built-in time tracking on Pro plan
  • Client portals: Share boards with external clients (Enterprise)
  • Sub-items and dependencies: Nested tasks with relationship tracking

HubSpot's task and activity management is solid for sales activities but doesn't approach Monday's project management depth. If your post-sale workflow involves onboarding, implementation, or ongoing account management, Monday CRM's project features are a genuine differentiator.

🏆
Winner: Project Management — Monday CRM (by a large margin)

Monday.com built its reputation on project management, and those capabilities are deeply embedded in Monday CRM. If collaboration, timelines, and project tracking matter to your team, Monday wins here emphatically.

Who Should Choose Which Platform?

Go HubSpot

Outbound prospecting, email sequences, pipeline management, and lead scoring are HubSpot's core strengths. Pure sales teams win with HubSpot.

Go Monday CRM

Teams that sell AND deliver work — agencies, consultancies, service businesses — will love Monday's combined sales + project management.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Monday.com a real CRM?

Yes. Monday CRM is a genuine CRM product with pipeline management, contact tracking, deal management, email integration, automation, and reporting. It originated from a project management platform, which gives it strong collaboration features. It's a different kind of CRM than HubSpot — more flexible and visual, less sales-automation-heavy. Over 186,000 companies use Monday.com for customer-facing workflows.

How much does Monday CRM cost in 2026?

Monday CRM pricing (annual, minimum 3 seats): Basic $12/seat/mo ($36/mo minimum), Standard $17/seat/mo, Pro $28/seat/mo, Enterprise custom pricing. Monthly billing is about 18% more. No free tier — 14-day free trial available.

Can Monday CRM replace HubSpot?

For basic pipeline and contact management, yes. For advanced email sequencing, marketing automation, and deep sales analytics, no. Teams that rely on HubSpot's marketing hub (campaigns, landing pages, SEO tools) or sophisticated sales sequences will find Monday CRM insufficient. Teams using HubSpot mainly for deal tracking may find Monday CRM a capable and cheaper replacement.

Does Monday CRM have email automation?

Monday CRM (Pro plan) includes email integration (Gmail/Outlook), email sending from within the CRM, and basic automation. It can send automated emails based on triggers. However, it lacks the depth of HubSpot's sequences — multi-step automated outreach with conditional branching, reply detection, and A/B testing.

Which is better for agencies?

Monday CRM is often the better choice for agencies because it combines sales pipeline with project management. You can track client deals AND manage client projects in one platform. HubSpot has a strong agency partner program and is widely used by marketing agencies for client management, but for pure workflow management across client acquisition and delivery, Monday is more versatile.

Does Monday.com integrate with HubSpot?

Yes. There is a native HubSpot integration on monday.com's marketplace that syncs contacts, deals, and other CRM data between the two platforms. Some teams use HubSpot for marketing automation and Monday for project management, syncing customer data between them. The integration is available on Standard plan and above.

Is Monday CRM good for small businesses?

Monday CRM is solid for small businesses that need a visual, simple system. The Pro plan at $28/seat is affordable for teams of 3–15. However, the minimum 3-seat requirement means solo operators pay for 3 seats they don't need. HubSpot's free tier is better for very small teams or solopreneurs just getting started.
