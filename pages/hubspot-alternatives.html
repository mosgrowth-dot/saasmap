The Real Reasons People Leave HubSpot

HubSpot consistently scores high in user satisfaction surveys, but there are clear scenarios where teams switch. Understanding the real pain points helps you make the right decision:

1. The Pricing Cliff

The most common reason people search for HubSpot alternatives is the pricing structure. HubSpot Starter is affordable ($20/user), but many of the features you actually want — full email sequences, advanced automation, forecasting, predictive lead scoring — require Professional at $100/user. That's a 5x price jump that surprises many teams.

2. Contact-Based Marketing Costs

HubSpot's Marketing Hub pricing scales with your contact database. If you have 100,000 contacts, your marketing costs can become significant even on paid plans. Competitors like ActiveCampaign and Zoho use different models that can be cheaper for large contact bases.

3. Limited Customization Below Enterprise

Custom objects — the ability to create data types beyond contacts, companies, and deals — are locked behind HubSpot Enterprise at $150/user. Zoho CRM offers custom modules from its Professional plan ($23/user). Salesforce has always had deep customization. Teams with non-standard data models often feel constrained by HubSpot's opinionated structure.

4. "I Just Need Sales, Not Marketing"

Many companies discover HubSpot is more platform than they need. If you're a 10-person sales team with no content marketing, the breadth of HubSpot feels like paying for a full kitchen when you just need a coffee maker. Pipedrive, Close CRM, and similar tools do sales pipeline better at lower cost.

⚠️ Before switching: Have you used HubSpot Free? Many teams on the free tier underestimate how capable it is and assume they need paid features they don't actually use yet. Run HubSpot Free for 60 days before committing to any paid CRM.

Should You Actually Switch From HubSpot?

Before switching CRMs, answer these questions honestly:

  • Are you on the free tier? If so, you're not actually paying HubSpot anything. Stay until you have a clear paid use case.
  • Are you on Starter ($20/user) and feeling limited? The right move may be upgrading to Professional rather than switching — a new CRM has migration costs.
  • Are you actually using HubSpot's marketing features? If your team only uses the CRM/pipeline and ignores email campaigns and landing pages, Pipedrive may be 50% cheaper for the same value.
  • What's your migration cost? Switching CRMs is never truly "free" — there's data migration time, workflow rebuilding, team training, and productivity loss. Calculate this before comparing sticker prices.
💡 Our honest take: If you're currently on HubSpot Free, staying is almost always the right call — you're getting one of the best free tools in B2B software. If you're on HubSpot Professional at $100/user and your team is using less than 50% of the features, then yes — Pipedrive or Zoho CRM will save you significant money for equivalent results.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best free HubSpot alternative?

The best free HubSpot alternatives: Bitrix24 (unlimited users free with CRM + project tools), EngageBay (free up to 250 contacts), Zoho CRM (3 users free), Freshsales (3 users free). Note that HubSpot's own free CRM is also excellent and worth evaluating — many "free HubSpot alternatives" have more limited free tiers than HubSpot itself.

What is cheaper than HubSpot but has similar features?

EngageBay is the most direct HubSpot equivalent at lower cost — CRM + marketing + service + chat in one platform, starting at $14.99/user vs HubSpot's $100/user for Professional. Zoho CRM Enterprise ($40/user) matches HubSpot Professional on most CRM features. ActiveCampaign often beats HubSpot on email marketing cost at comparable list sizes.

Is Salesforce better than HubSpot?

Salesforce is more powerful than HubSpot for enterprise-scale deployments, but it's also significantly more expensive and complex when you factor in implementation. HubSpot is better for SMBs, startups, and marketing-led teams. Salesforce wins for large enterprises needing extreme customization, territory management, and deep AppExchange integrations. See our full HubSpot vs Salesforce comparison.

How hard is it to migrate from HubSpot?

Migrating from HubSpot is moderately complex. Core CRM data (contacts, companies, deals, activities) export cleanly via CSV or API. More complex to migrate: marketing workflows, email templates, landing pages, form submissions, and attribution data. Most teams take 1–3 weeks to fully migrate. The biggest loss is typically historical marketing attribution data which is hard to transfer. Plan the migration carefully and run systems in parallel before cutting over.

Is there a HubSpot alternative with lifetime affiliate commissions?

Yes. EngageBay offers 30% recurring commission for life — one of the best affiliate programs in the CRM space. Salesflare also offers 30% lifetime recurring commissions. These contrast with HubSpot's 30% for 12 months. For affiliates, EngageBay's lifetime recurring model compounds significantly over time.
