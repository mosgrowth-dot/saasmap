What to Look for in a Free CRM

Not all "free" CRMs are created equal. Here's how to evaluate them:

  • Forever free vs trial: Some CRMs call a 14-day trial "free." We only include permanently free tiers.
  • User limits: Some free tiers cap at 1–3 users. A team of 5 can't use them.
  • Contact limits: Some limit the number of contacts you can store for free.
  • Feature depth: The free tier should include actual CRM functionality — not just a contact list with a "deals" button.
  • Data export: Can you export your data if you decide to switch? (Avoid CRMs that trap your data)
  • Upgrade path: If you eventually need paid features, is the upgrade affordable?
⚠️ The free CRM reality check: Free CRMs are legitimate business tools, but they have limitations by design — the goal is to get you to upgrade. Use a free CRM to validate that you'll actually use a CRM before paying. Most businesses can operate on free CRM for 12–24 months before hitting real limits.

When Should You Upgrade From a Free CRM?

Signs it's time to upgrade to a paid CRM plan:

  • You're missing follow-ups — manual tracking isn't enough, you need email sequences and automated reminders
  • Your pipeline lacks visibility — you can't easily answer "how is our pipeline doing this quarter?"
  • Team is hitting user limits — free plan caps mean someone on your team doesn't have access
  • You're running email campaigns — free tiers don't include mass email sending or marketing automation
  • You need integrations that require paid API access
  • Custom reporting matters — basic dashboards aren't giving you the insights you need
💡 Our recommendation: Start with HubSpot Free. Use it genuinely for 60–90 days. At the end of that period, you'll know exactly which features you're missing — making your paid CRM decision much clearer and better-informed. Don't buy features you haven't hit the limits on yet.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best free CRM software in 2026?

HubSpot CRM is the best free CRM for most businesses — unlimited users, the most capable free feature set, polished UI, and a clear upgrade path. Bitrix24 is best for teams needing free project management + CRM together. Zoho CRM and Freshsales are best for teams of up to 3 users that want more CRM-specific depth. EngageBay is best for testing an all-in-one free platform (CRM + marketing).

Is HubSpot CRM really free forever?

Yes. HubSpot CRM's core features are genuinely free forever for unlimited users. There's no time limit and no credit card required to start. HubSpot's business model is to provide an excellent free CRM and earn revenue from teams that grow and need paid features (automation, advanced reporting, marketing campaigns, AI tools). The free tier isn't disappearing — HubSpot has offered it since 2014.

What CRM can I use for free with unlimited contacts?

HubSpot CRM allows you to store 1 million+ contacts for free. Zoho CRM Free, Freshsales Free, and Bitrix24 Free also have no stated contact limit on their free plans (Bitrix24 has storage limits). EngageBay Free is limited to 250 contacts. Capsule CRM Free is limited to 250 contacts.

Is there a truly free CRM with email marketing?

EngageBay Free includes basic email marketing (newsletters, templates) for up to 250 contacts — the most capable free email + CRM combination. HubSpot's free CRM doesn't include mass email sending (that requires Marketing Hub, paid). Bitrix24 Free includes some email marketing features. For truly robust free email marketing + CRM, you'll need to combine a free CRM with Mailchimp's free plan (2,000 contacts free).

What's the difference between a free trial and a free tier?

A free trial gives you full access for a limited time (typically 14–30 days), then requires payment. A free tier (freemium) is permanently free with limited features. Pipedrive, Salesforce, and Close CRM have free trials but no permanent free tier. HubSpot, Zoho CRM, Freshsales, and Bitrix24 have genuine free tiers that never expire.

Can a free CRM work for a growing team?

Yes, up to a point. HubSpot Free works well for teams of 5–15 people managing active pipelines. The main limitations are: 200 email notification/month limit, no email sequences, basic automation only, and limited custom reporting. Most growing teams can operate on free for 12–24 months before hitting these limits. When you start losing deals because of missing follow-up automation, that's when to upgrade.
