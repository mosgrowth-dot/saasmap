Why People Leave Pipedrive in 2026

Pipedrive is a genuinely excellent sales CRM, and most users love using it. But there are clear scenarios where switching makes sense:

  • No free tier: Pipedrive starts at $14/user/month with no permanent free option. Startups and budget-conscious teams want a free starting point.
  • Limited marketing automation: Pipedrive's Campaigns add-on is basic. Teams doing email drip campaigns, landing pages, or ad tracking outgrow it quickly.
  • Growing feature needs: Teams that need territory management, advanced custom objects, or deep workflow automation often find Pipedrive's limits.
  • Team collaboration: Pipedrive is sales-focused but weak on collaboration. Teams that want project management alongside CRM look elsewhere.
  • Built-in calling: Pipedrive's calling requires integrations. Teams doing high-volume outbound calling prefer Close CRM or Freshsales with native calling.
  • Pricing concerns: Some teams find better value in Zoho CRM (more features per dollar) or HubSpot (free to start, better marketing).

How to Migrate from Pipedrive

Migrating your CRM data from Pipedrive is manageable with the right approach:

Step 1: Export Your Pipedrive Data

In Pipedrive, go to Settings → Data → Export Data. Export: Contacts, Organizations, Deals, Activities, Notes, and Products as CSV files. Pipedrive's export is comprehensive — you should be able to get everything out cleanly.

Step 2: Map Your Data Fields

Before importing, map Pipedrive's field names to your new CRM's fields. Most CRMs have bulk import tools that let you specify which column maps to which field. Custom fields in Pipedrive need to be created manually in the new CRM first.

Step 3: Import in the Right Order

Import in this order: (1) Contacts, (2) Organizations/Companies, (3) Deals (linking to contacts/companies), (4) Activities and Notes. This ensures relationship integrity.

Step 4: Rebuild Automations

Workflow automations and email templates need to be manually recreated in the new platform. Document your Pipedrive workflows before starting migration.

💡 Migration tip: Run the new CRM in parallel with Pipedrive for 2–4 weeks before fully switching. This ensures your data migration worked correctly and gives your team time to adapt without losing momentum on active deals.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best free alternative to Pipedrive?

HubSpot CRM is the best free Pipedrive alternative — unlimited users, deal pipeline, email tracking, meeting scheduling, forms, live chat, and basic automation at zero cost. Zoho CRM (free for 3 users) and Freshsales (free for 3 users) are also good free options. HubSpot's free tier is the most generous and capable of the three.

Is HubSpot better than Pipedrive?

HubSpot is better for teams that need marketing automation, free to start, or an all-in-one platform. Pipedrive is better for sales-focused teams that want a simpler, more focused pipeline tool at a lower price point than HubSpot Professional. See our full HubSpot vs Pipedrive comparison for the detailed breakdown.

Is Zoho CRM better than Pipedrive?

Zoho CRM provides more features per dollar than Pipedrive — enterprise-level capabilities at $40/user vs Pipedrive's Professional at $49/user, with more included at each tier. However, Pipedrive is significantly easier to use and has a more polished pipeline UI. Choose Zoho for value; choose Pipedrive for simplicity.

How long does it take to migrate from Pipedrive?

A basic Pipedrive migration (contacts, companies, deals, activities) takes 1–3 days for most teams. Complex migrations with custom fields, workflows, and large datasets (10,000+ contacts) may take a week. Run the new CRM in parallel for 2–4 weeks before full cutover to validate data integrity and allow team adaptation.

Why do people leave Pipedrive?

Top reasons for leaving Pipedrive: (1) Wanting a free CRM option — Pipedrive has no free tier, (2) Needing marketing automation beyond basic Campaigns add-on, (3) Growing team needs territory management, advanced reporting, or complex automation, (4) Wanting built-in calling for inside sales teams, (5) Finding better value in Zoho CRM, (6) Adding project management alongside CRM (Monday.com).
