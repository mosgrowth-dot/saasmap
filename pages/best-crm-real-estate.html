What Real Estate Agents Actually Need in a CRM

Real estate is a relationship business operating on extreme time pressure. Your CRM needs to solve specific problems that general business CRMs weren't built for:

Speed-to-Lead

Studies consistently show that leads responded to within 5 minutes convert at 21x the rate of leads responded to after 30 minutes. Your CRM must trigger automated responses the moment a new lead comes in from Zillow, Realtor.com, your website, or an open house. This is table stakes for top-producing agents.

Long-Term Nurturing

Most real estate leads take 3–24 months to convert. A good real estate CRM runs drip campaigns (called "Action Plans" in many platforms) that stay in touch with leads over months or years — through market reports, home anniversary emails, neighborhood updates, and personalized check-ins — without requiring manual effort.

Lead Source Aggregation

Real estate agents get leads from Zillow, Realtor.com, Homes.com, Facebook Ads, Google Ads, website forms, referrals, and open houses. Your CRM should aggregate all of these in one inbox and track where your conversions are coming from.

Transaction Management

Many real estate CRMs also handle the transaction side — tracking contract dates, inspection deadlines, and closing tasks. If yours doesn't, it should integrate with dedicated TC software like Dotloop, Skyslope, or Brokermint.

💡 The general CRM vs real estate CRM question: General CRMs like HubSpot and Salesforce are powerful, but lack real-estate-specific features like MLS/IDX integration, lead portal aggregation, and real estate drip campaign templates. Most high-volume agents choose a dedicated real estate CRM or use a general CRM with real-estate-specific integrations.

General CRM vs Real Estate-Specific CRM

Many agents wonder if they should use a general CRM (HubSpot, Pipedrive, Salesforce) or a real-estate-specific one. Here's the honest breakdown:

Use a real estate-specific CRM if:

  • You get leads from Zillow, Realtor.com, or other portals and need automated intake
  • You need long-term drip campaigns with real estate content built in
  • Speed-to-lead automation is critical to your business
  • You want MLS/IDX integration for property data
  • You need transaction management tied to your CRM

Use a general CRM if:

  • Your leads come primarily through your own website and content
  • You're tech-savvy and can configure integrations via Zapier
  • You want more powerful marketing automation than RE-specific tools offer
  • Your business model blends real estate with other services (property management, mortgages)
💡 The winning combo: Many top producers use Follow Up Boss as their CRM (for lead management and team operations) + HubSpot Marketing (for their personal brand content and email newsletter) connected via Zapier. Best of both worlds.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best CRM for real estate in 2026?

Follow Up Boss is the best CRM for real estate teams and high-volume agents — 250+ lead source integrations, excellent automation, and team management. LionDesk is best for solo agents on a budget at $39/month. Lofty is best for AI-powered lead nurturing. HubSpot is best for agents focused on content/digital marketing who generate their own leads.

How much does a real estate CRM cost?

Real estate CRM pricing varies widely: LionDesk ($39/mo), Wise Agent ($49/mo), Follow Up Boss ($69/user/mo), kvCORE (from ~$400/mo for brokerages). General CRMs like HubSpot have a free tier, and Pipedrive starts at $14/user/mo. Most solo agents spend $40–100/month on CRM; teams typically spend $200–1,000+/month.

What is speed-to-lead and why does it matter?

Speed-to-lead is how quickly you respond to new leads. Research shows leads responded to within 5 minutes convert 21x better than those contacted after 30 minutes. Real estate internet leads are competitive — the same lead may have been sent to multiple agents. Your CRM's automated instant response (text, email, or AI-powered message) ensures you don't lose leads to slower competitors.

Does Follow Up Boss work with Zillow leads?

Yes. Follow Up Boss integrates natively with Zillow (and was acquired by Zillow Group in 2023). New Zillow leads are automatically imported into Follow Up Boss and can trigger instant automated responses. Follow Up Boss also integrates with Realtor.com, Homes.com, Facebook Lead Ads, and 250+ other lead sources.

What real estate features should I look for in a CRM?

Essential real estate CRM features: automated lead response (speed-to-lead), drip campaign templates for long-term nurturing, lead portal integration (Zillow, Realtor.com), built-in texting/calling, mobile app, contact database with property data fields, and basic reporting on lead sources. Nice-to-have: AI lead qualification, video email, transaction management, and IDX website integration.

Can I use HubSpot for real estate?

Yes, but with limitations. HubSpot's free CRM provides excellent contact management, email tracking, and marketing automation. It's well-suited for agents who generate leads through content/SEO rather than portals like Zillow. HubSpot lacks native MLS integration, real estate drip campaign templates, and transaction management. Most high-volume agents prefer dedicated real estate CRMs like Follow Up Boss or LionDesk for their RE-specific workflows.
